# Midwifery practice environment: 2026 update analysis

A slide-by-slide revision guide for the American College of Nurse-Midwives' *Midwifery Practice Environment* presentation, which I prepared in February 2026 as ACNM's legislative advisor. The 2021 deck was still being shown to midwives and students with pre-Dobbs abortion scope data, a 2019 Medicare conversion factor, and maternal mortality figures that stopped in 2020. This document lists 17 changes in order of urgency, gives the replacement text or data for each slide, explains why the change matters, and cites every claim to a source published between 2022 and 2026.

The file is `2026-presentation-update-analysis.md`.

## Why it is here

This is the kind of document a policy advisor produces constantly and almost never shows anyone outside the organization: a careful, cited audit of material that is already in use. I am publishing it because it shows the work, and because the underlying findings (how far the practice environment moved between 2021 and 2026) are useful to anyone teaching or advocating in this space.

Everything in it is drawn from public sources. It is a snapshot as of February 2026; state abortion law and Medicaid policy in particular move quickly, and the trackers cited in Item 1 and Item 5 should be checked before any figure is reused.
